Automotive: common questions

What resolution dash cam do I actually need?
For most daily driving, 1440p (2K) at 30 or 60fps offers the optimal balance between clear license plate legibility and reasonable video file size without overheating issues in hot cabins.
Do OBD-II scanners work on every car?
Virtually all consumer passenger cars and light trucks sold in the United States and Canada since 1996 (and Europe since 2001) are OBD-II compliant, though proprietary ABS and SRS airbag codes may require vehicle-specific scanner protocols.
How do I find my car's paint code?
Your exact paint code is typically printed on the VIN driver's door jamb sticker, under the hood firewall, or in the glovebox manual. Never purchase a paint touch-up pen by color name alone.
