How to choose a robot lawn mower

Lawn size

Match the mower's rated cutting capacity to your total square footage, leaving 15-20% headroom for complex layouts.

Boundary wire or wire-free

Wire-free RTK satellite models map your yard digitally; wired models require burying a physical guide cable.

Slopes and obstacles

Check the maximum slope rating (e.g. 25% vs 45%) and whether the mower employs active optical obstacle sensors.

App and connectivity

Bluetooth handles local yard controls, while Cellular and Wi-Fi models enable alerts and remote scheduling away from home.

Frequently asked questions

Do robot lawn mowers work on slopes?
Yes, but maximum slope capability varies widely by model. Entry-level units manage slopes up to 25% (14 degrees), whereas high-traction AWD models can tackle hills up to 45% (24 degrees).
Are robot mowers really quiet?
Extremely quiet. Most electric robot mowers emit between 57 dB and 62 dB, comparable to a quiet office or gentle rain. You can run them at night or on Sunday mornings without disturbing neighbors.
Do I need a boundary wire?
Not anymore. Traditional budget models still rely on buried perimeter wires, but modern GPS RTK and AI camera-guided models map the boundary virtually via smartphone app.
