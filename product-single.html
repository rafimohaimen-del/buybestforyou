Questions

Will it match my car’s colour?
If matched to your vehicle's OEM paint code, solid factory colors match within 95%. Metallic and tricoat pearlescent shades require layered blending and will always show slight light variation.
Do I need the clear coat?
Yes. The color layer contains only base pigment. Without the clear protective top coat, the repair will dry dull and wash away after several rainfall and car wash cycles.
